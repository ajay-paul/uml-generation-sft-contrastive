The model contains two classes. One called 'TupleElement' and the other called 'Tuple'. It also defines a directed association that expresses that from an instance (objects) of 'TupleElement' associated 'Tuple' instances (objects) are reachable. One end of the association is labeled 'tuple' and carries a multiplicity constraint that expresses that the association relates each 'Tuple' object with exactly one 'TupleElement' object.
